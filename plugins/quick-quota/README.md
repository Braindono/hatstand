# quick-quota

Shows a one-line band above the prompt with your Claude subscription's 5-hour and weekly usage quota: a small meter, the percent used, and how long until each window resets. The figures come from the engine's own usage measurements (the same ones the status line uses), update after every reply, and turn amber at 70% and red at 90%, with a toast when a window passes 90%. Press Hide to dismiss it for the session. No setup; it reads nothing outside the session.
