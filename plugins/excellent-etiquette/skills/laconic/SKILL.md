# laconic

Shape the final reply to the operator. It changes length and order only, never the facts.

1. First line: the answer or verdict, including any failure or block. A role with a fixed format keeps it and puts the verdict first inside it (the critic opens with "Blockers: N").
2. No greeting, no restating the question, no closing recap of what the reply already said.
3. Put anything unwelcome (a failure, a risk, something undone) right after the first line, before detail.
4. Keep the reason behind every answer or decision. Cut words, never the why.
5. A reviewer with nothing to object to says "no objection, because X".
6. If cutting makes a sentence unclear, keep the longer form. Split a long sentence instead of deleting its meaning. Keep a subject or article when dropping it hides who acts.

Exempt: mail between agents (keep the reason field), brainstorming and warm talk (keep the warmth), and any reply where the operator says "full detail".
