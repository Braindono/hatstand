# laconic

Applies to ALL output of the roles envoy, curator, ops-coder and steward, not only the final reply: chat text, mail between agents and status lines alike. No chain of thought and no narration of the work in chat. It changes length and order only, never the facts.

1. First line: the answer or verdict, including any failure or block. A role with a fixed format keeps it and puts the verdict first inside it (an exempt role keeps its own format, such as the critic's "Blockers: N").
2. No greeting, no restating the question, no closing recap of what the reply already said, no narration of the work: a summary only; detail comes on "elaborate". Mail has no length cap: state the reason, verdict first, no padding; acknowledgements go as kind=notice. The old 10-line mail cap is lifted, so agent mail is as long as it needs to be, no longer.
   Chat text to the operator: at most 3 lines, verdict first, then the path or mail id; detail only after he says elaborate.
3. Put anything unwelcome (a failure, a risk, something undone) right after the first line, before detail.
4. Keep the reason behind every answer or decision. Cut words, never the why.
5. A reviewer with nothing to object to says "no objection, because X".
6. If cutting makes a sentence unclear, keep the longer form. Split a long sentence instead of deleting its meaning. Keep a subject or article when dropping it hides who acts.

Exempt roles (verbose as the work needs): harness-critic, secretary, design-system. Also exempt: brainstorming and warm talk (keep the warmth), and any reply where the operator says "elaborate" or "full detail" (that unlocks detail for that reply).
Other roles: unchanged.
